Set the phone down, tap Start, and leave it.

One tap of Start opens the camera and the microphone (iOS asks once), records everything you say as a storyline, and watches the camera: whenever something new appears in front of the camera and holds still — a page with a question, a note, a sketch — it is read within a couple of seconds and answered from your storylines (a check also runs every 15 seconds). Nothing to tap until you are done.

Listen and Ask are extras for when you do not want a full session: Listen records only (camera off), Ask takes one spoken question without recording. Start covers both.

Talk to it with the word Lens: "Lens, what does the toll-keeper take?" asks a question (a camera frame goes along), "Lens, look at this" reads what is in front of the camera, "Lens, new chapter" starts a new storyline, "Lens, stop recording" ends the session. Those sentences are not written into the transcript.

Once, before you start: — every storyline is then mirrored to a "Lens Loop" folder on your Drive.

First, add your Claude API key in settings (the gear at the top right).

    Settings

    The key is sent only to api.anthropic.com from this phone. Get one at console.anthropic.com (API keys) and set a monthly spend limit there. If you opened this page through a link that ends in #key=…, that link carries the key: bookmark it or add it to the Home Screen and you never type it.
    Model and effort start from the values in the page's code every time it opens; a change here lasts until the page is closed. max is the deepest reasoning and the slowest: a frame can take 30 s to several minutes and cost $0.10–0.60 or more in output tokens.
    Automatic. A speaking role-play card (a SETTING, a role such as DOCTOR or PATIENT, a TASK list): Claude shows a briefing — setting, your role, background, the task points. Say "I'm ready" and Claude becomes the role on the card for a live conversation: it stays in character, asks for information, raises worries from the card, and closes at about the length set below. Say "Lens, next" to end it early. A writing task (a letter, referral or report from case notes, over several pages): the case-notes pages are collected as you show them, and the page with the writing instructions brings the finished piece. The text on screen stays until a different task appears.
    Each sentence you say goes out the moment it is transcribed, so the model is already thinking while the pause runs; a further sentence inside the pause folds into the same turn. A reply still cannot arrive before the model has produced it: at low the first words land about a second after you stop talking; at max the model deliberates for ten to forty seconds first. The briefing runs at this effort too, so it is on screen within seconds; answers to anything else use the effort set at the top of the script.
    Lenses the phone reports are listed after the first Start. Zoom presets (widest, 1×, 2×) appear on the camera view while running.
    Google's sign-in lasts an hour on its own. With the one-file helper from drive-helper.gs deployed in your own Google account (it holds the OAuth client secret, which never goes in this page), the sign-in renews itself for ever, hands-free, on any browser. The page's own helper address is filled in already; paste a different one only after a new deployment, then tap Connect Google Drive once more.
    With a key, recordings and spoken questions are transcribed by ElevenLabs Scribe v2 Realtime (sent only to api.elevenlabs.io from this phone). Without one, the phone's own dictation is used. A link ending in #key=…&stt=… carries both keys.
    Your own glossary, sent ahead of the storyline names on every connection: the engine favours these spellings when it hears something close, which is what helps with invented words and with an accent. Up to fifty terms in all count.
    Listen records a storyline for as long as you talk — through ElevenLabs when a key is set, otherwise with the phone's own dictation (iOS asks for the microphone; keep Siri & Dictation on). Ask takes one spoken question and answers it from the saved storylines, picking the right one itself. Camera frames are answered with the storylines in mind too. Storylines are kept on this phone without a limit on their number or length; when they outgrow one request, the ones a question needs are chosen first and every other one is still known by its summary.
    Tilt is read from the motion sensor; iOS asks for permission on Start. Works best with Orientation Lock on, so Safari itself never rotates the page.

    Storylines

    Each recording is saved on this phone as a storyline with a title, summary and key terms written by Claude. Untick one to leave it out of answers. Export keeps a copy as a file; Import merges one back in.
